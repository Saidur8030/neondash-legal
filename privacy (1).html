This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your best score and your sound setting on your device only. We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contacts.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain information from your device to show ads and measure ad performance, such as your advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS), IP address, device type and operating system, app interactions, crash logs and diagnostics, and approximate location derived from the IP address. We do not receive your personal identity from these services.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

3. Your choices about ads

  • Consent: Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions), the Game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form, and lets you change your choice.
  • Android: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • iOS: On iOS the Game may ask for permission to track you. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

4. In-app purchases

The Game offers an optional purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We only learn whether the purchase was completed.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the advertising and store services described above, which operate under their own privacy policies.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game data (best score and settings) stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game. We do not hold an account database. For ad data held by Google, use the controls described in section 3 or Google's privacy tools.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the Game, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page with a new "last updated" date.
